About

Started with a
projector.
Now at Media Arts Lab.

Started as a projectionist convincing coworkers to make short films while saving up for Film School. Worth it.

Cut my teeth at my first agency gig as an editor and motion designer, then moved up to Head of Video Integration — Bacardi and Howard Stern campaigns, late nights, impossible deadlines, zero complaints.

Packed up and moved to LA. Found my people fast — writers, directors, troublemakers — and got to work. Traveled the world with Red Bull and SPEED. Picked up a job at Chiat\Day on Visa and Nissan, then started my own company.

Currently at Media Arts Lab, serving as Senior Creative Editor — crafting the visual and social voice of the world's most loved brand.
